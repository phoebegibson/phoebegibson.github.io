About

Since then, I've worked at a newspaper, for a nonprofit, and most recently, for myself as a freelance writer. You can find my words in publications like Christianity Today, Chron.com, and Houstonia Magazine, and for organizations like AboveBoard, Level All, and the University of Houston Law Center.
