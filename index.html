About

I'm Phoebe, like from Friends. I studied journalism at Baylor University where I learned AP Style and how to conjugate verbs in Arabic. Since then, I've worked at a newspaper, for a nonprofit, and most recently, for myself as a Houston-based freelance writer. You can find my words in publications like Christianity Today, Chron.com, and Houstonia Magazine, and for organizations like AboveBoard, Level All, and the University of Houston Law Center.
